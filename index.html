About

I'm a full-stack developer who enjoys turning everyday business problems — booking a hall, ordering food, managing a hotel front desk — into software that people actually want to use. My work spans the whole stack: React and vanilla JavaScript on the front end, Python and PHP on the back end, and PostgreSQL or MySQL underneath.
